Undergraduate · World models & embodied intelligence本科生 · 世界模型与具身智能

I am an undergraduate in the Department of Automation at Tsinghua University. My research interests lie in world models and embodied intelligence. My recent research explores how agents use visual observations and language to understand human intent and anticipate the effects of actions when instructions are ambiguous or incomplete.

Beyond research, I take part in technology competitions, student leadership and service, and fieldwork in China and abroad. Through robotics competitions, event organizing, campus initiatives, and international exchange, I continue to learn through hands-on work and collaboration with people from different backgrounds.

我是清华大学自动化系 2024 级本科生，研究兴趣是世界模型与具身智能。最近的研究关注在指令存在歧义或关键信息不完整时，智能体如何结合视觉观察与语言指令理解人的意图，并预测物理世界的变化。

研究之外，我也参与科技竞赛、学生工作与社会实践。从机器人竞赛与赛事筹备，到校园事务和国内外实践调研，这些经历让我有机会在技术实践、团队协作和跨文化交流中不断学习。

Publications论文

NeurIPS 2026Main Track · Poster主会 · Poster

AmbiguousWorld: Benchmarking and Resolving Ambiguous Instructions in Video World Models

Bo Wang*, Sen Cui*, Jin Li†, Mingxi Huang†, Zhikang Chen, Min Zhang, Guocai Yao, Changshui Zhang

Project page项目主页 Code代码

AmbiguousWorld addresses instructions that leave key details unstated. At inference time, a vision–language model decomposes instructions into subgoals, explores candidate video branches through tree search, and prunes those that violate task or physical constraints.

We build an ambiguous-instruction benchmark on DROID and evaluate the framework with Cosmos, Wan and CogVideoX, improving weighted generation quality across all three video backbones.

针对省略关键细节的指令，AmbiguousWorld 在推理阶段引入多模态树搜索：由视觉语言模型拆分子目标、评估候选视频，并筛除偏离任务或违背物理约束的分支。

我们基于 DROID 构建歧义指令基准，在 Cosmos、Wan 和 CogVideoX 三个视频模型上评估该框架，均改善了加权生成质量。
